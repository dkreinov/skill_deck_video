## Nine indicators proposed by this pass

1. Non-human research workflow autonomy (METR time horizons, public vs internal).
2. Automated experimentation and pipeline output (tokens/PRs per researcher; 8x merged code).
3. Dynamic benchmark and evaluation synthesis velocity (Epoch Capabilities Index).
4. Wall-clock interval reduction in generational rotations.
5. Algorithmic authorship of production code and research literature.
6. Pre-training compute-to-capability elasticity (prescriptive scaling sigmoids).
7. Energetic requirements per unit of cognitive capability.
8. Insurance and capital-market pricing of AI capability risk.
9. Frontier-lab hiring and compensation composition.

Each carries a causal link, current evidence, publisher, confounders, an explicit
falsification condition, and a strongest source. **Novelty is not evidence**: indicators are
carried into the deck only where the evidence behind them survives the evidence matrix.
Indicators 8 and 9 rest on recruiting-database and insurance-marketing sources and are
treated as *speculative* class; they appear in the video, if at all, as "things to watch",
never as measurements of self-acceleration.
